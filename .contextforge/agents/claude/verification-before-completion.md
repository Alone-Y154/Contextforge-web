## Verification Before Completion

Never claim work is complete, tests pass, builds succeed, bugs are fixed, or requirements are met without running the verification command and attaching its output as evidence in the same response.

**Gate function:** (1) Identify proof command, (2) Run it fresh and complete, (3) Read full output and exit code, (4) Verify claim matches output, (5) Only then make the claim.

**No exceptions:** "should work," "I'm confident," "linter passed," and "agent reported success" are not evidence. Partial checks prove nothing. Spirit over letter—rephrasings of success claims still require evidence.
